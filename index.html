Tools for Scout leaders

Free apps that take the paperwork out of running a section, made by a Scouts Australia leader. They run in your browser, with nothing to install.

Beacon

Phone-first helper for Terrain

OAS sign offs, progress and unit insights from your Terrain records: who's close to finishing what, what to programme next, and quick sign offs after a unit night.

  • Record OAS
  • Progress
  • Insights
  • Approvals
  • Works offline
Open Beacon

Program Builder

Term programs for Scout families

Turn a term's activities into a tidy one-page program for families. Meeting dates and public holidays fill in for you, and it always fits on one page.

  • One-page PDF
  • Calendar file
  • Spreadsheet import
  • Every section
Open Program Builder

Snag Calc In progress

Sausage sizzle shopping and restock

Plan a fundraising BBQ: how many sausages, bread, sauce and drinks to buy for the serves you expect, the cheapest packs at each store, and what to restock for day two.

  • Shopping list
  • Day 2 restock
  • Cost and profit
  • Spreadsheet copy
Open Snag Calc

Pinewood Control

Run a pinewood derby race night

A big-screen kiosk for the standings and a phone to run each heat. Elimination scoring, late entrants and drop-outs, and a popular vote with a countdown reveal.

  • Live kiosk
  • Pair by QR code
  • Popular vote
  • Reusable racer lists
