Tools for Scout leaders

Free apps that take the paperwork out of running a section, made by a Scouts Australia leader. They run in your browser, with nothing to install.

Beacon

Phone-first helper for Terrain

OAS sign offs, progress and unit insights from your Terrain records: who's close to finishing what, what to programme next, and quick sign offs after a unit night.

  • Record OAS
  • Progress
  • Insights
  • Approvals
  • Works offline

Program Builder

Term programs for Scout families

Turn a term's activities into a tidy one-page program for families. Meeting dates and public holidays fill in for you, and it always fits on one page.
